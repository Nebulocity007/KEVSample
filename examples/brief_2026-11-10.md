## Method and Confidence
Source: [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) (public). Relevance is scored by transparent rules (watchlist match, internet exposure, ransomware use, recency); ATT&CK techniques are rule-mapped. The watchlist's product choices and weights are analyst judgment; each entry cites KEV CVEs showing the product is exploited, but none shows how widely financial institutions run it. KEV confirms exploitation *somewhere*; it does not confirm targeting of financial institutions, so no judgment here is rated above what the source supports.
